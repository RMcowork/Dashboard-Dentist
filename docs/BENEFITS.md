# Benefits for a dental clinic

What the dashboard does for the people who run a clinic. For the exact screens and KPI formulas, see [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).

## For the owner or managing dentist
- **See how the clinic is doing at a glance:** revenue, money collected, completed visits, new patients, no-show rate and how busy the chairs are. Each number is compared with the previous period.
- **Catch lost income:** outstanding balances and the gap between billed and collected show where money is slipping away.
- **Compare the team:** revenue, visits and no-shows per dentist show workload and performance side by side.
- **Spot trends early:** charts show revenue over time, which treatments bring in the most, and whether no-shows are rising.

## For the front desk
- **Today's schedule by chair**, with a live status for each patient: booked, arrived, in the chair, done or no-show.
- **Ready-made call lists:**
  - Tomorrow's appointments to confirm, which reduces no-shows.
  - Unpaid balances to collect.
  - Patients due for a recall (no visit in over 6 months and nothing booked), which brings back patients who would otherwise drift away.
- Phone numbers are tap-to-call.

## Practical advantages
- **Always current:** it refreshes automatically from Airtable, every minute by default, and highlights what changed.
- **Works anywhere:** on a reception screen, a tablet or a phone. On a phone the schedule becomes a simple time-ordered list.
- **Suits the staff:** English, Hebrew and Arabic (right-to-left), amounts in ₪, and a light or dark theme.
- **Low cost and easy to adopt:** it runs on Airtable's free plan with nothing to install. Clinics can try it on demo data first and then connect their own records.
- **Careful with patient data:** it only reads data, never changes it. It shows just names and phone numbers where needed, and the access token isn't stored in the code or on GitHub.

## In short
The owner gets a clear picture of the business, and the reception gets a practical to-do list for the day, both from the same up-to-date data.
